Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the websites, bots and other projects published by ShibunyanTeam (the "Services") process, how it is used and what choices you have. ShibunyanTeam is the controller of this information. Contact details are at the end of this page.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the Service, we may process:

  • platform identifiers: Discord or Twitch user IDs, usernames, avatars, server and channel IDs, and role information;
  • message content and events handled in real time to provide moderation and automation features, such as commands, links or flagged content;
  • configuration and logs: settings chosen by server or channel owners, moderation actions and audit records;
  • technical data: IP address, request logs and error reports created by our servers when you use a website or API;
  • messages you send to us directly, such as emails.

We do not intentionally collect payment card data, government identifiers or precise location.

2. How We Use Information

  • to provide, operate and maintain the Services;
  • to apply the rules and settings chosen by community owners and to detect abuse;
  • to keep the Services secure, debug problems and improve reliability;
  • to respond to your questions and requests;
  • to comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal Bases (EEA and UK)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on legitimate interests (operating and securing the Services), performance of a contract (providing a feature you requested), consent where we ask for it, and legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • the platform the Service runs on, such as Discord or Twitch, as part of normal operation;
  • infrastructure and hosting providers acting on our behalf;
  • authorities, when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

5. Data Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Server configuration is kept while a bot remains in the server and may be deleted after it is removed. Logs are kept for a limited period. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as access controls and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights or to request deletion, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity first. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The Services are not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and Third-Party Resources

This website does not use cookies or analytics trackers. To display the page, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and the avatar image from GitHub. These providers may receive your IP address and browser details under their own privacy policies.

10. International Transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located in a different country than you. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, write to shibunyan0@gmail.com.
